NOTES
- Goods LP in 1950 equals one only because both data and model LP series are indexed to 1950.
  It is a normalization/check, not an additional identifying moment.
- eta_ns is fixed at one; eta_m, rather than eta_ns, is calibrated.
- theta_m and theta_rs are recovered internally using the two 1950 VA shares as anchors.
- Model VA shares are omega_j s_j / sum_i omega_i s_i, omega_j = 1-(1-alpha) mu^y_j mu^x_j.
- mu^y_j = LS_j/(alpha + (1-alpha) LS_j mu^x_j), with LS_j the labor share of sectoral value added.
- Several blocks fit complete annual paths, so identification is not a one-parameter/one-scalar-moment count.
- Sector prices are separately indexed to one in 1950 before their endpoint ratios are computed.
- Data prices are relative to the private VA deflator (Tornqvist). The chained model rows divide
  by a Fisher chain of model sector prices with VA weights; the final-good rows use the model numeraire.
- Relative service prices are imposed through the production wedge tau^p, so they never test the model.
- Model capital prices are data capital prices times D, the chained VA deflator in final-good units.
- LP targets are the smoothing-spline trends of log LP: Goods fits the annual trend path, and
  kappa_rs and kappa_ns equate the model and trend slopes of cumulative log LP since 1950.
